## Findings and action
- The hub pages naturally mention connected modules. Their scope remains broader; a short description and specialist link is useful, not automatically harmful duplication.
- No competing duplicate primary title/H1 intent found in these 13 pages. Each has one H1 and an index/follow policy.
- Exact paragraph scan (main content only, 12+ words, collapsed whitespace) found only the repeated online-only FAQ answer across feature pages. Shared navigation, client band, plan links and product names are expected reuse. This check cannot detect all semantic duplication or search cannibalization.
- Tracking versus Attendance: keep the team map/date-wise route/activity detail on Tracking; check-in/out, working hours and late/partial records on Attendance. Revised Attendance links to Tracking instead of expanding route supervision.
- Invoice versus Collection versus Tally: invoice document, receipt/outstanding review and accounting mapping are distinct. Payment entry does not establish bank clearance. Free receipt/reminder tools do not update the business account.
- Van Sales versus Delivery: ready-stock selling and closing stock are distinct from fulfilling an already confirmed shipment. Existing pages explain that distinction.
- Production versus Target Meter: factory materials/cost/output and commercial sales achievement remain separate.
- Removed concrete defects in the two target pages: fabricated/unverified testimonial, impossible credit-limit example, fixed collection improvement claim, exact unsupported alert schedule, and absolute anti-fraud/rollout promises. Illustrated arithmetic is now consistent.
- Other 11 approved feature HTML files are unchanged by this draft, including Order Taking and Salesman Tracking. Their existing interaction/math checks remain part of the full suite.
- Broader operational issue: saleson.co.in has separate hosting and Payment Collection currently redirects to /404. A repository/Vercel update alone does not fix that canonical-domain gap. Coordinate the approved files and route with the main-domain host.
- Product-specific claims retained from existing content still benefit from first-hand demo confirmation and approved customer evidence. No claim that every feature or business outcome has been independently certified.
