Choose the part of the business you need to improve

A manufacturer with an established factory system may need field orders and distributor collections first. Another may need to review production costing alongside finished stock. Use your own brief to select modules rather than assuming every workflow needs replacing.

  • Sales owner: customer access, prices, demand and territory review.
  • Dispatch team: available stock and the delivery handover.
  • Accounts team: invoices, outstanding balances and the required Tally records.
  • Production team: BOMs and work-order/costing requirements, where relevant.

Prepare a manufacturer-specific demonstration

Bring one distributor order, a finished-goods stock example and the related invoice. Add a BOM and batch-cost sheet only if production is part of the brief. Ask who creates, reviews and changes each record.

Confirm godown structure, users and permissions, connectivity, accounting versions and data migration before agreeing rollout timing. SalesOn requires internet and does not support offline use.

Start small and confirm the full scope

SalesOn has a ₹0 setup fee and a minimum of 2 users. Choose monthly or annual billing on the current pricing page; GST is extra as applicable. Van Sales is Enterprise functionality. Discuss onboarding, training and support arrangements using the workflows you plan to run.

Is this a replacement for every manufacturing ERP?

No. The published modules establish specific production and sales workflows, not universal ERP parity. Demonstrate specialist procurement, planning, quality, compliance or integration requirements before committing.
