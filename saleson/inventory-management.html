Keep warehouse stock and van stock distinct

Van Sales has its own load, sale, return and closing-stock review. It is included in Enterprise. If goods move from a godown to vehicles, ask the team to show your actual movement and reconciliation process rather than assuming a loading sheet alone updates inventory.

Use free planning tools for a separate task

The free reorder calculator helps plan quantities; the purchase order tool prepares a document. They do not automatically create a cloud inventory receipt or purchase bill. Product inventory is the operational workflow evaluated in SalesOn.

What should we check before rollout?

  • Product list, selling units, opening stock and godown structure.
  • Whether batch, expiry, serial-number or specialist controls are needed; confirm the exact supported scope.
  • The user who can change stock records and how exceptions are reviewed.
  • Your accounting transaction types and Tally configuration.
