What are SalesOn Free Business Tools?

SalesOn Free Business Tools are a set of 12 free, browser-based tools for Indian distributors, wholesalers, manufacturers and MSMEs. Each tool is designed for a specific document or calculation task in the daily operations of an Indian trading or distribution business. You can create GST invoices, quotations, delivery challans, payment receipts, purchase orders, payment reminders, stock reorder plans, e-way bill checks and van loading sheets — all for free, with no signup required.

Who can use these free tools?

  • Distributors managing daily orders, billing and van sales
  • Wholesalers creating invoices and quotations for retail buyers
  • Manufacturers generating POs and delivery challans for dispatches
  • FMCG businesses managing route-level van sales and stock
  • Pharma distributors creating GST invoices with HSN codes
  • Retailers billing customers with payment receipts
  • Service businesses using GST SAC codes for invoicing
  • Field sales teams tracking payments and outstanding dues
  • Van sales teams managing load-in, delivery and EOD reconciliation

Why use free tools before software?

When you are just starting out or running a small operation, free tools are a practical starting point. They let you create professional documents without investing in software, learning a new system or training staff. SalesOn Free Tools are designed with the same logic and field-tested document formats that larger businesses use — so when your volume grows and you move to SalesOn, the transition feels natural.

When should you move to SalesOn?

Move from free tools to SalesOn when you start experiencing: too many invoices to handle manually, manual WhatsApp payment follow-up consuming your time, stock mismatches between warehouse and van, no visibility of salesman or delivery status, payment collection delays from field teams, Tally or Busy double-entry slowing your accounts, and no consolidated business reports. SalesOn solves all of these in one connected platform.
