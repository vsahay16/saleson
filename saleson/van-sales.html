03 / Back at the office

Close the route.
Explain the difference.

A good closing review answers two separate questions: does the stock count make sense, and where is the money from the day’s sales?

  • Review stock by itemCompare the expected balance with the count. Separate saleable returns from damaged goods.
  • Review collections separatelyCheck cash and digital receipts, credit sales and customer dues. Sales value is not the same as cash in hand.
  • Resolve exceptions before sign-offTrace differences back to the load, sale, return or receipt instead of carrying an unexplained balance into tomorrow.

Van sales software helps a salesperson sell directly from stock carried on a vehicle. SalesOn connects the van’s stock record with outlet sales, invoices, receipts and returns, so managers can review the route’s stock and collections.

In van sales, the salesperson carries ready stock and sells at the outlet. Order Taking records demand for later fulfilment. Delivery Management handles shipments and trips for confirmed orders. Choose the workflow that matches how the goods reach your customer.
