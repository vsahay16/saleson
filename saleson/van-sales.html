Van Sales Software — FAQ

Van sales software automates the ready-stock route-sell cycle — the van carries goods, the DSR sells and raises a GST invoice at each kirana or general trade outlet, collects Cash or UPI on the spot, and reconciles all stock and cash at end of day. SalesOn covers every step from morning load-out to accounts sync — no paper bills, no manual counting.

At end of day, the DSR taps "Close Day." SalesOn auto-matches opening stock loaded in the morning against total units sold, returns, and damaged goods — per SKU. Any gap is flagged instantly.
