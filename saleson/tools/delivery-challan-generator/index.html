Help with this tool

Check the consignor, consignee, goods and quantities before dispatch. Transport and signature settings can be expanded when needed.

The challan and e-way bill serve different purposes. Use the related checker to review the movement; this generator does not submit anything to the GST portal.

💡

When is a delivery challan used? Delivery challans are commonly used when goods are moved without issuing a tax invoice, such as stock transfer, job work, goods sent for approval, delivery before invoice, demo/exhibition movement, or van loading. Please verify GST compliance with your accountant.
