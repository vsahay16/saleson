Background

However, their sales tracking and data management processes were inefficient. The Challenge:

Before using SalesOn, Lapcare faced multiple operational bottlenecks:

  • Inefficient Communication : Coordinating between the sales team and back office via WhatsApp resulted in miscommunication and lost information.
  • No Performance Insights : The company had no reliable way to measure sales trends, leading to missed growth opportunities.

The Solution:

SalesOn helped us address inefficiencies by providing :
