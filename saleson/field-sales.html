Field sales questions.
Clear, practical answers.

What is field sales management software?

Field sales management software organises the work salespeople carry out at customer locations. SalesOn connects route-wise customer access, outlet information, recorded activity and sales actions with a web workspace for manager review.

How is field sales management different from salesman tracking?

Field sales management covers the wider working day: customer information, sales activities and manager reporting. Salesman tracking focuses on employee location and movement history. Both can support different parts of the same field team’s workflow.

Can reps take orders and record collections during visits?

Yes. The connected Order Taking module supports product selection, customer-wise pricing, supported schemes and order capture. Payment Collection supports records against unsettled invoices, including partial or full collections.

Can managers review daily activity on the web?

Yes. Authorised managers can review synchronised field records and recorded activities for a selected day. They can review connected transactions when an order, customer or collection needs follow-up.

Can field users add new customers and outlets?

Yes. Field users can create customer or outlet records with a map location. After synchronisation, authorised users can access the information for later visits and follow-up.

Does the field sales app work offline?

No. SalesOn requires an internet connection and does not support offline use.

How should we choose a plan and prepare for rollout?

Choose the users and modules your team needs, prepare your customer and product lists, and arrange training. See current pricing or book a demo to plan your rollout.
