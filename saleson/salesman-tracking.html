How to choose a salesman tracking app

Check these four things with a typical day from your own field team:

  • One map with employee names and latest location times.
  • Employee-wise route history for a selected date.
  • Distance and total field time in the day summary.
  • Customer visits and recorded sales activity in sequence.

A salesman tracking app gives authorised managers a view of their field team’s recorded locations and daily movement. SalesOn provides a team map, date-wise employee routes, distance, total time and a timeline of recorded field activities.
