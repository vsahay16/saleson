## Interpretation and measurement

The exports contain three distinct comparison windows repeated four times. Property clicks changed -8.3% over six months, -1.8% over three months and +8.8% in the latest 28 days. Tools gained visibility; a site-wide collapse or a tools-caused decline is not established. Page-family counts combine URL variants and do not equal property aggregation. Exact custom date ranges, daily series and query-filtered page tables were absent.

After release, record the deployment date. Compare consistent 28-day windows and year-over-year periods for these canonical page families. Segment brand/non-brand and device. Export query-to-page data before merging pages or claiming cannibalisation. Track completed registrations and completed demo bookings by landing page/source/device in analytics; a CTA click or locally saved lead is not a completed conversion.
