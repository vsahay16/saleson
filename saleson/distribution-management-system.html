Before you get started

DMS questions.
Clear, practical answers.

Bring a typical stock and customer transaction to your demo. Check how your distribution process fits SalesOn.

What is a distributor management system (DMS)?

A DMS helps managers organise stock, sales and customer accounts. SalesOn brings secondary sales, multi-godown stock and customer transactions into one back-office workflow.

What is the difference between primary and secondary sales?

Primary sales are manufacturer-to-distributor transactions. Secondary sales are distributor-to-retailer or dealer transactions. These do not show consumer purchases unless retailer-to-consumer transactions are separately captured.

Can SalesOn handle multiple godowns?

Yes. SalesOn DMS supports stock visibility across multiple godowns so managers can compare product availability by location.

Can we review batch and expiry information?

Yes, for products managed with batch and expiry records. Check the controls required for your product categories during the demo.

How does DMS connect with ordering, invoicing and collections?

SalesOn connects DMS with Order Taking, Invoicing and Payment Collection. Your team can review customer orders, billing records and collections alongside stock and customer accounts.

How is DMS different from field sales management?

DMS focuses on distribution records and back-office control. Field sales management focuses on customer visits and the sales team’s daily activity.

What should we prepare for a DMS demo?

Bring your product list, godown structure and a typical sale or return. The team can walk through your process, reporting needs, user access and onboarding.
