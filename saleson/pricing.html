How much does SalesOn cost?

SalesOn Pro costs ₹208 per user per month on the yearly plan (₹2,500/user/year) with no setup fee. Enterprise is ₹333 per user per month on the yearly plan (₹4,000/user/year). Monthly billing is also available at ₹250 (Pro) and ₹400 (Enterprise) per user per month. There is no minimum number of users and no hidden charges. A 7-day free trial is available with no credit card required.

Does SalesOn charge a setup fee?

No. SalesOn charges ₹0 setup fee. There is no implementation cost, no onboarding charge, and no annual commitment unless you opt for annual billing to get the discount. Dedicated onboarding, account manager, and salesman training are included in every plan at no extra cost.

Can small teams start with SalesOn?

Yes. SalesOn has no minimum user requirement. A team of 2 can start on the same day with full features. There is no annual lock-in on monthly billing. As the team grows, you simply add users. The pricing scales with actual usage only.
