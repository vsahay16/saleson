# Authentic SalesOn workflow recording brief

## Deliverable

One approved 45–60 second screen recording from the actual product, showing the same sample business and transaction throughout. Use a dedicated training account with no customer, employee, payment or login secrets. This is a recording brief, not a claim that the recording exists.

## Suggested sequence

1. Open an example customer and select one product.
2. Enter quantity and price, then save one order successfully.
3. Create its invoice and show the matching customer and total.
4. Record an actual sample payment through the configured product process and show the outstanding balance.
5. Open the relevant stock view and explain when that configured workflow posts a stock movement.

Have the product owner confirm the real transaction sequence, tax example, configured stock behaviour and successful saved records. Do not simulate these screens in HTML or stitch unrelated accounts together. Avoid showing OTPs, notifications, personal contacts or bank details.

## Caption outline

- Select a customer and record the order.
- Review the invoice created for that order.
- Record the payment and check the balance.
- Review the related stock records.

Match captions to the final recording's actual timings, and revise the wording if the configured process differs. Do not generate a guessed caption track or upload date.

## Placement and loading

Use the existing homepage product-video slot, replacing the introduction only after approval. Keep the introduction available if useful within an existing page; do not create overlapping video pages. Use relevant excerpts on feature pages only when the excerpt actually demonstrates their task. Preserve click-to-play, a compressed local poster, preload=none and no autoplay. Add captions from the approved recording, a verified duration and publication timestamp when released. A small video cannot reasonably meet an 80 KB image budget; compare visual legibility before choosing compression settings.
