Manufacturing questions.

What is production management software?

Production management software connects a product’s bill of materials, work orders, material consumption, production costs and finished output. It helps factory owners and production managers review what is planned, what is in progress and what a batch costs.

Who is SalesOn Production designed for?

It is designed for manufacturers managing repeat products, multi-stage production or assembly, with teams that need a shared view of materials, work orders, costs and finished stock.

Can SalesOn manage a multi-level bill of materials?

Yes. A multi-level BOM connects raw materials and sub-assemblies to the finished product. Managers can review the component quantities and costs behind a production order.

How are work orders and production orders connected?

A work order defines the finished product and quantity. Production orders organise the sub-processes and their teams, so managers can follow the stages and review their costs against the overall work order.

How does batch costing work?

Batch costing brings the recorded material, labour and overhead costs together for a production run. Dividing that cost by the finished output gives the cost per unit. Scrap and changes in output can affect that result.

Can production connect with stock, sales and Tally?

Yes. SalesOn connects production with finished goods inventory and its sales workflows. Tally Integration provides the accounting connection. Review the transaction types and mappings your accounts team needs during the demo.

Does SalesOn Production work offline?

No. SalesOn requires an internet connection and does not support offline use.

What should we bring to a manufacturing demo?

Bring one product BOM, a typical work order, your stock locations and a batch cost sheet. We can walk through that example with your production and accounts teams, then discuss users, pricing and onboarding.
