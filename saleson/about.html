The company behind the product

One product. A connected working day.

SalesOn is developed and operated by Swarnshlok Software Private Limited, an Indian software company. Our focus is the day-to-day work of businesses that sell and distribute products.

A salesperson’s order matters to more than one person. The office needs the details, the stock team needs to act on it and the manager needs a clear view of progress. We design SalesOn around those connections.

Our product approach

Make daily work easier to follow.

01

Start with the task

Design around the order, customer visit or stock movement a team needs to complete, with the information for that task close at hand.

02

Connect field and office

Help salespeople record work on their phones and give the office a shared view of the records they need to manage.

03

Keep people in control

Business administrators manage team access. Managers review the activity and records available to their account.

SalesOn requires an internet connection and does not support offline use. Device compatibility is listed in the app stores.
