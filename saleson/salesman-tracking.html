02 / Review one salesperson’s day

Read the route.
Then read the activity.

Choose the date and look at movement alongside the events recorded in SalesOn. Give your next conversation a clear starting point.

  • Distance and total time

    Use the day summary to understand the recorded travel and time for that employee.

  • Customer activity in sequence

    See when visits, orders, payments and invoice updates were recorded. Keep the event connected to its customer and time.

  • A specific follow-up

    Ask about the relevant route segment or customer event, rather than asking the salesperson to reconstruct the whole day from memory.
