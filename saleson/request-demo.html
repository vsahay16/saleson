Questions People Ask
Before Booking

What happens in a SalesOn demo?

A SalesOn demo is a focused 30-minute live walkthrough of the features most relevant to your role — van sales, DMS, field sales tracking, or billing. We show you real workflows on a live account, answer your specific questions, and give you an honest assessment of whether SalesOn fits your business.

How long does setup take after the demo?

Rollout timing depends on your data, workflow and training requirements. SalesOn requires zero IT infrastructure — no server, no installation beyond the Android app. Your dedicated onboarding manager configures your products, team, and routes. Salesmen get personal training before Day 1.

Can SalesOn tell us honestly if it's not a fit?

Yes. If SalesOn is not the right fit for your workflow, team size, or budget, we will tell you directly during the demo — and suggest what you actually need. We don't run aggressive sales pitches or push businesses into a tool that won't work for them. Our Refund policy applies backs that up.
