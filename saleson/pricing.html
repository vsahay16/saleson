Before subscribing

Pricing questions, answered.

Need to confirm a workflow or payment detail? Talk to SalesOn.

How many users do I need?

Paid subscriptions start with 2 users. The calculator applies this minimum to both plans.

Which plan includes van sales?

Van sales and end-of-day settlement are included in Enterprise. They are not included in Pro.

Is there a setup fee?

The setup fee is ₹0. Discuss your account preparation and training needs with SalesOn before starting.

How does annual billing work?

Annual billing is paid for a year upfront. The monthly equivalent shown on the cards is a comparison figure, not a monthly payment schedule.

Are taxes included?

Prices are in INR. GST is extra where applicable. The calculator excludes taxes; confirm the final invoice amount before purchase.

Can I try SalesOn before subscribing?

A 7-day free trial is available with Pro features. If you need Enterprise workflows such as van sales or production, book a demo to discuss them.

What are the cancellation and refund conditions?

Read the Terms of Use and Refund Policy before purchasing. Cancellation does not automatically mean a refund; eligibility and exclusions are described in the policy. Terms of Use · Refund Policy.

Can customers outside India use this pricing page?

This is the single SalesOn price list, shown in INR. If you need payment or tax details for a business outside India, contact SalesOn before subscribing.
