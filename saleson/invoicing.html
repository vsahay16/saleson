What is GST invoicing software?

GST invoicing software helps a business prepare sales invoices with customer details, item quantities, prices and tax amounts. SalesOn supports mobile billing, invoice sharing, printing and credit-note adjustments for distributors and manufacturers.

How is invoicing different from taking an order?

An order records what a customer wants to buy. An invoice is the billing document for the sale. Use Order Taking for catalogue search, customer-wise pricing and scheme selection; use Invoicing to review and issue the bill.

How does SalesOn calculate GST on an invoice?

SalesOn calculates tax using the product rates and transaction details entered in your setup. It supports CGST/SGST and IGST calculations. Your team remains responsible for choosing the correct classification, rate and transaction treatment.

Can we share and print invoices?

Yes. Invoices can be shared as PDFs on WhatsApp and printed in supported A4/A5 or thermal formats. For mobile thermal printing, check your printer model, paper width, device and connection with the SalesOn team before rollout.

Are a PDF invoice and an e-invoice the same thing?

No. A PDF is a document format. An e-invoice workflow involves registration and its associated identifiers. SalesOn supports e-invoicing and e-way bill workflows; ask the team to demonstrate your setup, registration response and document output during the demo.

Can we apply a credit note to an invoice?

Yes. SalesOn provides an Apply Credits to Invoices view, showing the credit note, available credits, invoice amounts and dues. A credit adjustment and a cash refund are different actions; review the intended treatment with your accounts team.

Does invoicing include payment follow-up or Tally setup?

Invoice creation is covered here. Use Payment Collection for outstanding balances and receipts, and Tally Integration for accounting connections, transaction mappings and sync requirements. DMS and Van Sales cover their wider stock workflows.

Does SalesOn work offline?

No. SalesOn requires an internet connection and does not support offline use.

Is this the same as the free GST invoice generator?

No. The free generator is a separate tool for creating an individual invoice. The SalesOn product page covers the invoicing workflow used by your business team. See current plans or book a demo to choose the setup you need.

What should we bring to an invoicing demo?

Bring a recent invoice, your item and customer lists, a credit-note example and the printer model you use. Your billing and accounts teams can review document formats, GST report exports and any e-invoicing or accounting requirements together.
