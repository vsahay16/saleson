Quick Answer: What is the SalesOn mobile app?
SalesOn is a mobile app for field sales teams to take orders, track visits, collect payments, handle GST billing and manage van sales. Owners and managers monitor all activity from the SalesOn web dashboard.
Quick Answer: How do salesmen log in?
Salesmen log in using a username and password created by their company admin in SalesOn. They do not create their own public account. The business owner must register on SalesOn first.
Quick Answer: Should the owner register first?
Yes. The business owner or decision-maker registers and sets up the company account on SalesOn before their sales team can use the mobile app. Request a free demo to get started.

How It Works

How the SalesOn mobile app flow works

The app works within a structured business account. Here is the full flow — from owner registration to field team execution.

1
🌐
Owner discovers & registers on SalesOn
The business owner or decision-maker visits saleson.co.in, requests a demo, and registers for a free trial. This is the starting point — the app works only for businesses on SalesOn.
2
🔑
Admin receives company account access
After registration, the company admin gets login access to the SalesOn web platform at app.saleson.co.in to configure the business — products, customers, routes, pricing and billing.
3
👥
Admin creates salesman and field staff users
The admin creates individual user accounts for each salesman or field staff member. Usernames, passwords and role permissions are all configured by the admin — not the salesman.
4
📲
Salesmen download the app
Field salesmen download the SalesOn app from Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). The app is available on both platforms for users assigned under an active SalesOn company account.
5
🔐
Salesmen log in with assigned credentials
Salesmen open the app and enter the username and password given by their company admin. There is no separate public sign-up. Access is entirely company-controlled.
6
📈
Field activity flows into SalesOn
Orders, visits, payments, GST invoices, van sales, delivery updates and EOD reports all flow into SalesOn through the SalesOn dashboard — visible to managers and owners on the web dashboard.

Important: Salesmen do not need to create a separate public account from the app. Access is created by the company admin after the business starts using SalesOn. There is no self-registration from the mobile app.

New to SalesOn? Set up your business account before asking your sales team to download the app.
