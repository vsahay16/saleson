03 / Review the result

A sent record
still needs a clear result.

Keep the source transaction and accounting entry traceable. When a record needs attention, give the office a clear owner for the follow-up.

  • Review the posting result before treating the handover as complete.
  • Check mappings when a customer, item or ledger needs correction.
  • Agree how retries, duplicates and edited records are handled.

Review the Tally company, exact software release, connector or connection method, access permissions and availability of the Tally environment. Walk through a successful posting and a record needing correction. Agree backup, reconciliation and support responsibilities before rollout.
