From distributor order to dispatch.

1

Record the distributor’s demand

Open the distributor account and capture the required products and quantities with the applicable customer prices.

2

Review finished-goods availability

Have the office compare the order with stock by godown. If more output is needed, the production team reviews its separate planning and work-order process.

3

Bill and follow the handover

Prepare the invoice, follow dispatch in Delivery Management and review collections with the distributor account.

The order is larger than the stock ready to ship

The sales team records the distributor’s demand. The office compares it with finished goods by godown and agrees the quantity available for supply. Production reviews any additional output through its own BOM and work-order process, while dispatch and accounts follow the confirmed sale.
