Review the sale before preparing the bill

Keep the customer details, selected items, quantities and prices together while reviewing the order. Use the catalogue and order workflow to establish what the customer wants; use Invoicing to prepare the billing document.

A repeat-customer example is useful: find the correct product, review the price, confirm quantity and prepare the invoice with your billing user.

Share or print the invoice

SalesOn’s published invoicing workflow supports PDF sharing and A4/A5 or supported thermal formats. Your team chooses the correct GST setup and transaction treatment. For thermal printing, check the printer model, paper width, phone and connection before rollout.

A PDF invoice and a registered e-invoice are different. If registration is required for your business, ask for the relevant supported setup and response to be demonstrated.

Check your counter requirements before choosing

This page brings together supported order, invoicing, stock-review and collection functions. It does not establish a dedicated retail POS, consumer ecommerce checkout or payment-gateway integration.

  • Bring your usual invoice and printer details.
  • Test your product identification, units and any barcode requirement separately.
  • Confirm cash-counter, return and payment processes with the team.
  • Test internet availability; SalesOn does not support offline use.
