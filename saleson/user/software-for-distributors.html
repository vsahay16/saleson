Test a changed order before you decide

Bring a customer order with several product lines, one unavailable item and a partial payment. Ask the rep and office to follow the same example from order review to the final bill and outstanding balance.

Agree who can change quantities, review stock and authorise exceptions. Confirm customer prices, godown structure, opening data and user access before setting a rollout date. SalesOn requires internet and does not support offline use.
