One claim. Three responsibilities.

Give every expense
a clear next step.

Keep the submission, the approval and the reimbursement handover connected, with a clear responsibility at each stage.

  1. 01

    The salesperson submits

    Record the expense category and amount, add the relevant details and attach a photograph of the bill from the mobile app.

  2. 02

    The manager decides

    Review the claim against policy limits. Check supporting information and approve or reject with a comment. Route claims through additional approval levels when needed.

  3. 03

    Accounts prepares reimbursement

    Use approved expense records for the reimbursement review. Keep the approval decision separate from the payment your accounts team makes.
