The company behind the product

From Quintol to SalesOn.

At Quintol, the founders saw how an order passed through sales, stock, dispatch and accounts. Gaps between those steps made the working day harder to coordinate.

They brought that operating experience and their technical background into SalesOn, building software around the records and handovers a distribution team needs each day.
