Guide

How to Use This
Purchase Order Generator

1. Enter Your Company Details

Add your company name, GSTIN, address and upload your logo. This appears on the purchase order as the buying party — the company placing the order.

2. Add Vendor / Supplier Details

Enter the vendor's name, GSTIN, address, contact person and phone. The vendor is the party receiving and fulfilling this purchase order.

3. Fill PO Details

Set PO number, PO date, expected delivery date, delivery location, payment terms and shipping terms. PO number is auto-generated but fully editable.

4. Add Items with GST

Add each product with HSN/SAC code, quantity, unit, rate, discount and GST rate. Taxable value, GST amount and total are calculated automatically per row.

5. Enable Approval & Delivery Schedule

Turn on the approval section to add prepared by, checked by and approved by fields. Add a delivery schedule for phased or batch deliveries.

6. Download PDF or Share

Download PDF, print, or share via WhatsApp. Use "Convert to Stock Note" to save PO data for later inward stock entry connection with your inventory system.

Difference

Purchase Order vs Invoice
vs Delivery Challan

Document Who Issues Purpose Has GST?
Purchase Order Buyer to Vendor Intent to purchase — confirms what, how many and at what price Optional (reference only)
GST Invoice Seller to Buyer Demand for payment — official GST tax document after supply Yes — required
Delivery Challan Seller to Buyer Movement of goods — accompanies physical delivery Optional (movement only)
