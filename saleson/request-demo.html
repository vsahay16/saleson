Before you book

Demo questions, answered.

Is the demo free?

Yes. You can book a free 30-minute SalesOn walkthrough before deciding whether to subscribe.

Can my colleagues join?

You can share your booking details with colleagues who manage sales, distribution, accounts or operations. Bring the people who need to evaluate your workflow.

Do I need to upload business data?

No upload is needed to book. A description of one daily task is enough. If useful, bring a sample order, stock sheet or report with sensitive details removed.

What if the calendar is slow or does not appear?

Use the “Open booking calendar” link beside the calendar to open the same SalesOn scheduler in a new tab. Choose an available time and finish the booking there.

How soon can we start after the demo?

Discuss the suitable modules, subscription and onboarding steps during the call. Setup timing depends on your products, opening balances, team access and training needs. See the pricing page for current plans.
