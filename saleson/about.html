Why We Built SalesOn

The problem was never
technology. It was access.

In 2017, a small FMCG distributor in Maharashtra came to us with a problem we kept hearing everywhere: his salesmen were writing orders on paper, billing from memory at day's end, and spending two hours every evening reconciling registers that still didn't add up.

Software existed to solve this. But every option either cost ₹2–3 lakh in setup fees, required a minimum of 20 users, or was so complex it needed a dedicated IT person to run it. None of it was built for a 5-van distributor in Nagpur.

"Enterprise distribution software was priced to filter out the businesses that needed it most."

So we built SalesOn — not a stripped-down "lite" version of expensive software, but a genuinely complete distribution platform designed from scratch for Indian field conditions, Indian workflows, and Indian business sizes. The same software a 200-van FMCG company uses. Available to a 2-person distributor. From day one.
