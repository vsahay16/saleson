Before your demo

Sales target questions.
Direct answers.

What is sales target tracking software?

Sales target tracking software compares assigned goals with recorded achievement over a set period. SalesOn Target Meter helps managers and salespeople review progress, remaining gaps, rankings and configured incentives.

How does Target Meter help manufacturers?

Manufacturers can give sales heads, regional managers and field teams a shared view of sales goals and achievement. Product-category and team-level reviews help managers see where a sales conversation or follow-up needs attention.

What types of sales targets can we track?

SalesOn supports target tracking across products, salespeople, teams and territories. Examples include sales value, item quantity and productive calls. Bring your target sheet to the demo to match each measure with the correct sales records.

Can we distinguish primary and secondary sales targets?

Primary sales are manufacturer-to-distributor transactions. Secondary sales are distributor-to-retailer transactions. Agree which transaction stage counts toward each target so the two stages are not added together as the same sale.

How do leaderboards and incentive tracking work?

Leaderboards help teams compare recorded performance. Incentive tracking shows progress against configured reward rules. Define the qualifying transactions, thresholds, period and treatment of returns before using the result for incentive review.

Is the incentive example on this page an actual payout?

No. The interactive example uses sample achievement percentages and reward points to explain threshold-based rules. It is not a live account, payroll calculation or payment commitment.

Is this for factory production targets or sales targets?

This page covers sales target tracking and incentive visibility. Use SalesOn Production for BOMs, work orders, material consumption and batch costing, and DMS for distribution stock and customer transactions.

Does SalesOn Target Meter work offline?

No. SalesOn requires an internet connection and does not support offline use.

What should we bring to a target tracking demo?

Bring a monthly target sheet, your salesperson or territory structure, the product categories you track and one incentive plan. We can review the setup with your sales manager and discuss current pricing and onboarding.
