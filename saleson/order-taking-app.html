What is an order taking app for salesman?

A mobile app that replaces paper order books. Field reps search any SKU in under a second, schemes auto-apply, and a digital order confirmation is sent to the retailer's WhatsApp — no setup fee.

How fast can a salesman book an order?

A trained rep books a complete order in 1–3 minutes per outlet. SKU search returns in under a second. Schemes apply automatically. One tap confirms — before the rep leaves the counter.

Every trade scheme calculates at line-item level automatically. Credit limit warnings block over-credit orders. Customer-wise pricing prevents wrong rates. Three causes of order errors, eliminated.

Does it work for small distributors with 2–5 salesmen?

Yes. No minimum user count, no setup fee. Works for 2-salesman operations and scales to large teams. Pricing is per active user — you pay only for actual usage.
