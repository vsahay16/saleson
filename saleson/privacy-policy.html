Introduction

This privacy policy sets out to explain how SalesOn uses and protects any information that you may share when using saleson.co.in, its browser tools, enquiries and demo scheduling, as well as the SalesOn mobile app ("App"). SalesOn is operated by Swarnshlok Software Pvt. Ltd. The website-specific sections below explain browser storage and third-party services separately from App registration and transactions.

You may be required to submit from time to time certain information for the purpose of registration and carrying out transactions on the App, which may be personally identifiable and of utmost necessity for transacting on the App. SalesOn shall use this information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

SalesOn shall, at its sole discretion, amend this Privacy Policy from time to time. It is your responsibility to review the current policy. The original effective date is January 1, 2022; website coverage was updated on October 28, 2026.

App registration and transaction information

We will collect personally identifiable information from you in a variety of ways, including but not limited to:

  • Personal details like name, email address, and contact number
  • Business information like category of business, billing information, and shipping information
  • GSTIN, which may be mandatory to upload on the App for our verification and records, in order to give you further access for transacting on the App

What do we do with personally identifiable information?

Market research and business analytics. Your personally identifiable information may be used for market research and business analytic activities undertaken by SalesOn to measure various parameters of its business. SalesOn may also provide data collected from you — including name, email, phone number, and information collected while you access our App such as device information, location, and network carrier — to third-party service providers, only to personalize the App to your requirements and/or use third-party analytical tools for behavioural analytics, solely to improve your user experience. Third-party website services and the information they receive are described below. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

Business transfers. In the event SalesOn completely assigns or sells its core business, the buyer shall be entitled to use your information as per the provisions of this Privacy Policy.

Legal compliance. Where it is of utmost necessity for SalesOn to divulge your personally identifiable information to comply with applicable laws, court orders, decrees or proceedings, or governmental or regulatory rules and regulations, or for arbitration purposes as directed by the legal framework of India, SalesOn shall in good faith — without your express permission — release your information to the appropriate authorities.

Third-party processors. Where we use third-party processors to administer and process for the purposes notified in this Privacy Policy, we may share details with third parties such as auditors, legal advisors, and other professionals to obtain advice. Any such processing is governed by an agreement in the form required by law, preserving any statutory data protection rights.

Cookies and technical information

When you visit the website or load a service embedded in it, the website host and service providers can receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device details, page URL, referring page and request time. Cookies and similar identifiers may help measure visits, remember preferences and connect activity across visits. IP addresses and online identifiers can relate to a person or device; they are not treated as automatically anonymous.

Browser local storage and session storage are separate from cookies. The sections below explain their use for tools and preferences. This notice describes those uses; it does not mean that a cookie-consent choice has been collected.

Website analytics and advertising

The website includes Google Tag Manager and, on some pages, Meta Pixel, Microsoft Clarity and LinkedIn Insight Tag. Tag Manager can load additional measurement tags according to the site's configuration. These services support traffic measurement, understanding page interactions and advertising measurement; advertising tags may also support audience matching or remarketing.

Depending on the service and its configuration, providers can receive page visits, referral information, browser or device information, online identifiers and interaction events. Clarity can collect session-replay and interaction data. Do not assume that every input is masked or that browser-saved information is inaccessible to scripts running on the page.

The tools' event helper limits its own event fields to page or tool identifiers rather than contact details. This does not describe every tag loaded by Tag Manager or guarantee that other providers receive no personal information.

Provider information: Google, Meta, Microsoft and LinkedIn.

Browser storage and drafts

Some tools save drafts and document details in localStorage on the browser you use. Depending on the tool, this may include your business name, GSTIN, customer or supplier names, addresses, contact details, items, prices and payment information. Some enquiry forms also save contact details and a local record that the form was completed. Tool handoffs save selected information so another SalesOn tool can use it.

Preferences may also be stored, such as the quiz language or a demo selection. localStorage can remain across visits until it is removed; sessionStorage normally lasts for the browser tab's session. A handoff's age checks do not automatically erase every saved draft.

Browser storage is not an account backup or a private vault. People with access to the same browser profile, and scripts allowed to run on the page, may be able to access it. Avoid entering sensitive customer or payment information on a shared device. Use a tool's reset or clear control where available and your browser's site-data controls to remove remaining stored data. Clearing cookies alone may not remove localStorage.

Tools, documents and enquiries

The free tools use the information you enter to calculate results, prepare documents and support selected handoffs between tools. Downloading, printing or sharing creates a separate copy under your control. Opening a WhatsApp reminder or sharing a document with another service sends the selected information to that service or recipient under its own terms.

Where you complete an enquiry or demo-interest form, the details can include your name, phone number, business name, city and workflow requirements. They are used to respond to your enquiry and discuss SalesOn. Some tool forms save these details in the browser. If a lead-submission endpoint is configured, the form also sends the details to that endpoint for follow-up. Saving a form locally is not confirmation that SalesOn has received it or that a demo is booked.

Browser-based processing does not mean a page makes no network requests: analytics, externally loaded services and any configured enquiry endpoint can still receive information as described in this policy. Enter only information you are authorised to use and avoid unnecessary personal details.

Demo scheduling and embedded services

The demo page embeds a zcal calendar and links to the same scheduler in a new tab. Loading the embedded calendar can send technical information to zcal before you submit a booking. When you complete a booking, zcal processes the contact details, chosen time and any answers you provide; SalesOn receives booking information needed to arrange and follow up on your demo. See zcal's privacy policy.

Other pages may load YouTube videos, Google Fonts or other external resources. Loading an embed or resource can disclose technical information to its provider even without clicking an outbound link. Information you submit through external services is also governed by their policies. Share a description or a redacted example for a demo rather than confidential customer records.

Retention and sharing

Browser drafts remain on your device according to the storage behaviour described above. Downloaded or shared copies remain wherever you save or send them. Clearing browser data does not delete copies already sent to SalesOn, a scheduling provider or another recipient.

Enquiries and booking details are used for the requested contact, scheduling and related business records. Retention depends on the purpose, any ongoing customer relationship and applicable recordkeeping needs. External services apply their own retention settings and policies. Contact us for information about a particular record or to request deletion; this policy does not promise a fixed deletion period or that we can remotely erase your browser's data.

Website hosting, analytics, scheduling and communications providers may process information outside your country. App-related business transfers and legally required disclosures are described above. Ask us about the services or records relevant to your request.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can use browser settings to block or remove cookies, clear site data and restrict third-party content. Blocking these services may affect tools, preferences, videos or scheduling. Browser controls do not withdraw a message or booking already submitted.

For questions, access or correction requests, deletion requests or follow-up preferences, email support@saleson.co.in. Tell us which service and record you mean without sending passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary identity documents. We may need information to verify and locate the relevant record, and will explain any limits on the action we can take.

Links to other websites

You should be aware that while on our website or App, you could be directed to other apps or websites where the personal information collected is outside the control of SalesOn. For example, clicking on a banner advertisement may take you off the App. The privacy policy of the new website will govern the information obtained from you on such app or website.

Changes to this policy

We may change this Policy and our Terms & Conditions from time to time and will post the latest version of this Privacy Policy on this website and, where relevant, the App. If we ever wish to use your personal information in a way that is incompatible with this Privacy Policy, we will only do so with your consent.
