What should a delivery challan include?

A complete delivery challan protects both consignor and consignee and keeps the transporter clear on what's moving where.

Consignor & Consignee Details

Names, addresses, GSTIN and state of both the sender and the receiver of goods.

Challan Number & Date

A unique, sequential reference number and the date goods were dispatched.

Reason for Transport

Why the goods are moving — job work, stock transfer, approval, exhibition or van loading.

Item & Quantity Details

Description, HSN/SAC code, quantity and unit of every item being transported.

Vehicle & Transport Details

Transporter name, vehicle number, mode of transport and e-way bill number if applicable.

Receiver Acknowledgement

A signature and date box so the receiver can confirm goods were received in good condition.

When is a delivery challan used under GST?

Under Rule 55 of the CGST Rules, 2017, goods can be moved on a delivery challan instead of a tax invoice in specific situations, including:

Job Work

Sending raw material or semi-finished goods to a job worker for processing.

Stock Transfer

Moving stock between two godowns, depots or branches of the same business.

Goods Sent for Approval

Sending goods to a buyer who may accept, reject or return them after inspection.

Delivery Before Invoice

Dispatching goods when the tax invoice will only be issued after delivery is confirmed.

Exhibition / Demo Movement

Carrying goods to an exhibition, trade fair or customer site for a demonstration.

Van Loading

Loading stock onto a delivery van before retail-point billing happens on the route.

Sample Movement

Sending product samples to a prospective customer, distributor or retailer.

Replacement / Return

Moving goods back to the consignor for replacement, repair or return.

Delivery Challan vs GST Invoice vs E-Way Bill

These three documents work together during goods movement but serve different purposes.

Document Purpose Compliance Note
Delivery ChallanDocuments goods movement without a sale, e.g. job work or stock transferNot a tax invoice; carried in triplicate as per Rule 55
GST InvoiceOfficial tax document issued for a taxable supply of goods or servicesMandatory legal GST document; creates tax liability
E-Way BillElectronic document required for movement of goods above the prescribed valueGenerated on the e-way bill portal; accompanies the invoice or challan
