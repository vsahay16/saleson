Everything you need to know about Payment Receipts

Answers to common questions for Indian distributors, wholesalers, and MSMEs.

📖 How to use this Payment Receipt Generator

Fill in your business name, address, and GSTIN. Then enter customer details and receipt number. Select payment mode (Cash, UPI, Cheque, Bank Transfer). Enter amount received and total invoice amount. Balance due and payment status are calculated automatically. Download PDF or print directly from your browser.

👥 Who is this for?

Designed for Indian distributors, wholesalers, manufacturers, FMCG companies, pharma distributors, field sales teams, van sales teams, and MSMEs who need to issue professional payment receipts to customers for collections against invoices, advances, and partial payments.

📋 What should a payment receipt include?

A proper payment receipt should include: receipt number and date, business name with GSTIN/PAN, customer name, payment mode, transaction reference, amount received, invoice or order reference, balance due, payment status, and authorized receiver signature.

🔄 Difference between invoice and payment receipt

An invoice is issued by the seller requesting payment from the buyer. A payment receipt is issued after the payment is received, acknowledging the collection. A GST invoice records the supply of goods/services. A payment receipt records the money collected against that supply.

📊 How to track partial payments and balance due

Enter the total invoice amount and amount received. This generator automatically calculates the balance due and shows the correct status badge — Partial, Balance Due, Paid, or Overpaid. For ongoing outstanding, add previous outstanding to the formula and track it party-wise using SalesOn.

🚀 When to move from free tools to SalesOn?

If your business collects payments from more than 20 parties a month, has multiple salesmen collecting in the field, needs to track outstanding party-wise, or requires daily collection reports — it's time to move from free tools to SalesOn's integrated distribution platform.
