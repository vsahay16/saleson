Feature Module 1

See unpaid invoices and customer balances.

See outstanding by invoice — not just party total. Every rupee is aged into 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, and 90+ day buckets so your team chases the right accounts first.

  • Invoice-level aging: 0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ day buckets per party. Prioritise the oldest, largest first.
  • Partial payment tracking: Outstanding reduces on each payment. Remaining invoices stay open until fully settled.
  • Agent assignment: Assign outstanding to field agents by area or route — clear ownership per collection.
  • Party account view: Full history per customer — invoices, payments, receipts — in one screen.
