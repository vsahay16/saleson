Guide

How to Use This
GST Invoice Generator

1. Enter Seller Details

Add your business name, GSTIN, address, state and state code. Upload your company logo — it appears on the invoice. All data stays in your browser.

2. Add Buyer Details

Enter the customer's name, GSTIN (optional), billing and shipping address, state and state code. The tool uses state codes to auto-detect CGST/SGST vs IGST.

3. Fill Invoice Details

Set invoice number, invoice date, due date, place of supply and payment terms. Invoice number is auto-generated but editable.

4. Add Line Items

Add products or services with HSN/SAC codes, quantity, unit, rate, discount percentage and GST rate. Tax is auto-calculated.

5. Download or Print

Click Download PDF. The invoice opens in browser print — choose Save as PDF. Only the invoice is printed, not the website UI.

6. Share via WhatsApp

Click WhatsApp Share to generate a payment reminder message with invoice amount and balance due. Share directly to the customer's WhatsApp.
