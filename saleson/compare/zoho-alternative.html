When to shortlist Zoho CRM or SalesOn

Zoho CRM advertises a free edition for three users. The reviewed page describes contact follow-up and mobile access, while paid editions add broader CRM capabilities. Professional explicitly lists invoices, sales and purchase orders, products and vendors. [1]

Consider Zoho CRM if contact, lead and deal management are your starting point, or its paid CRM capabilities match your wider process. Shortlist SalesOn when the brief centres on field ordering, distributor operations and collections. Do not assume Zoho lacks orders or invoicing; match the edition and workflow.

Why compare a paid tool with a free CRM?

If a free CRM handles the whole job, it can be the more economical choice. If your business needs additional operations, price the specific paid edition, connected applications and setup work. SalesOn’s value is an accessible operational starting point with published prices, not a claim to undercut a zero-cost CRM or replace every Zoho application.

Follow one existing customer through two different needs

  1. Log a prospect follow-up and move the deal to the next stage.
  2. Use the same customer for an itemised repeat order and invoice.
  3. Record a partial collection and review the remaining customer balance.
  4. Verify which edition or connected application owns each record and how staff avoid duplicate entry.

SalesOn needs an internet connection and does not support offline use. Confirm the required permissions, support arrangements and integration scope during your evaluation.

Zoho CRM comparison questions

Does Zoho CRM lack inventory and invoicing?

No. The reviewed Professional edition lists inventory-related records and invoices. Confirm the edition, operational depth and accounting requirements rather than treating these functions as absent.

Is SalesOn’s two-user minimum unique among small-team products?

It is a useful entry point, but not a reason to claim every competitor has a larger minimum. Zoho CRM advertises a free edition for three users; compare the functions your team needs.
