Delivery Management — Frequently Asked Questions

Delivery management software automates the last-mile dispatch cycle for pre-confirmed orders — route sequencing, driver assignment, digital proof of delivery, and COD reconciliation. For Indian FMCG distributors, it replaces manual WhatsApp dispatch and paper challans with a structured, cloud-connected execution layer.

Delivery management software fulfils pre-confirmed orders — dispatch planning, route sequencing, POD, and COD collection. Van sales software handles sell-and-deliver from vehicle stock — on-spot invoicing and van inventory management. SalesOn supports both as separate modules.

Three options: OTP sent to the retailer's mobile, geotagged photo taken at the outlet, and digital signature on the driver's app. All three create a GPS-tagged, timestamped cloud record linked to the GST invoice.

Drivers log payment mode and amount at each stop. At day end, SalesOn compares collections against invoice values, flags discrepancies by outlet and amount, and produces a clean reconciliation report for the accounts team — posted at day close. No manual entry at the accounts desk.

When a delivery cannot be completed, the driver selects a structured reason — outlet closed, COD declined, or address mismatch. The stop is automatically queued for re-attempt in the next beat cycle.

Instant account activation — most distributors are live and dispatching on Day 1. Setup, accounts connection, and first route run the same day. Driver training takes 45–60 minutes. No setup fee, no minimum users, 7-day free trial.
