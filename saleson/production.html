Multi-Level Bill of Materials — How It Works

Define the full component structure for every product — raw materials, packaging, sub-assemblies, and semi-finished goods — each with quantities, expected scrap %, and cost. Total unit cost rolls up automatically across all BOM levels and recalculates when raw material prices change.

  • Nested Sub-Assemblies: Unlimited nesting — semi-finished goods feed into master product BOMs for food, pharma, or electrical production.
  • Auto Unit Cost: Raw materials, packaging, labor, utilities, and overhead computed per unit. Updates instantly on price changes.
  • Expected Scrap per Level: Wastage % defined at each BOM stage, rolled into cost calculations, and compared against actual per work order.

Production Management Software —
Common Questions

Production management software tracks the full manufacturing cycle — raw material issue, batch execution, scrap recording, and finished goods posting. For Indian manufacturers it replaces Excel with real-time BOM-based batch control. Every production event generates an auditable record with input quantities, output units, scrap, yield, and per-unit cost.

A multi-level BOM includes nested sub-assemblies and semi-finished goods — each with raw material quantities, scrap %, and cost. Single-level BOMs only list direct ingredients. Multi-level is essential for FMCG, pharma, cosmetics, and electrical assembly where products pass through intermediate production stages. Total unit cost rolls up automatically across all levels.

SalesOn calculates full absorption costing per batch — raw material at current purchase price, packaging, direct labor, utilities, and overhead. On batch closure, actual vs planned variance shows which element caused the gap. Per-unit cost is locked on the batch record and posted to warehouse inventory automatically.

Actual scrap is recorded against the BOM-expected scrap % for every batch. Yield percentage and rupee variance are calculated automatically on batch closure. Products consistently over expected scrap are flagged across batches — helping you catch process issues before they compound.

Yes. Every batch closure posts finished goods to warehouse inventory and makes production summaries ready for your accounts team — with no manual entry required.
