When to shortlist Freshsales or SalesOn

Freshsales’ paid-plan page lists contact stages, chat, email, phone and workflows. Higher editions add sales sequences, territory management and forecasting-related tools. These are relevant CRM capabilities; they should not be dismissed as features only large companies need. [1]

Freshsales is worth evaluating when lead follow-up, communication and deal progression drive your process. Shortlist SalesOn when repeat customer orders, distribution records and payment follow-up are the centre of the working day. A business that needs both should define the handover between systems before buying.

A free CRM can be sufficient for a different job

Freshworks’ official pages do not present the free option consistently: its main paid-plan page and its plan-comparison page differ. Confirm availability directly. A free contact-and-deal workflow can cost less than SalesOn; establish whether it also covers your required order, billing and collection process before comparing totals. USD prices here are not converted to INR.

Try a lead and a repeat retailer order separately

  1. Follow a new prospect through contact, follow-up and a deal stage.
  2. Place a repeat retailer order, then show the invoice and unpaid balance.
  3. Identify any additional product, integration or manual step between those records.
  4. Compare the required paid editions, add-ons and user counts—not a free CRM against a different operational scope.

SalesOn needs an internet connection and does not support offline use. Confirm the required permissions, support arrangements and integration scope during your evaluation.

Freshsales comparison questions

Is SalesOn always cheaper than Freshsales?

No blanket claim is made. Freshworks lists a free CRM option on a separate official comparison page. Compare the paid modules, billing term and operational requirements you actually need.

Should a distribution business replace its CRM with SalesOn?

Only if the required CRM process is covered. Test prospect tracking and communication separately from repeat ordering and collections; the two jobs may require different systems.
