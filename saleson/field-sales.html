Direct Answers

What Field Sales Software Should Help You Manage

Start with three questions: which outlets should each rep handle, what information do they need at the outlet, and how will the manager review the day?

What is field sales management software?

It is a mobile and web system for organising the daily work of sales employees outside the office: authorised customers, outlet information, activity capture, connected sales actions and manager reporting.

What should a field sales app contain?

A useful field sales app should give the rep customer context before the action, a simple way to record the action, and automatic synchronisation so the manager sees the same data.

Is it the same as tracking or order booking?

No. Field sales management is the broader workday layer. GPS proof belongs to Salesman Tracking; detailed SKU ordering belongs to the Order Taking App.

📝 Daily Field Activity Log

Capture the Field Day as It Happens — Not at 7 PM

The value of field sales software is not another form for the rep to fill. It is a usable, date-wise record that reaches the manager while the information is still current.

  • User-wise timeline: Review the activities performed by each employee for a selected date.
  • Customer-linked records: Keep activity information attached to the relevant customer or outlet.
  • Mobile-to-web sync: Data recorded in the field becomes available to authorised managers on the web platform.
  • Connected transactions: Orders, collections and other specialist actions can be opened through their dedicated SalesOn workflows.

Field Sales Management Software — FAQ

Field sales management software is a mobile and web system for organising the daily work of sales employees who operate outside the office. It gives reps access to assigned customers, outlet information and sales actions, while managers receive structured activity records and reports instead of relying on calls, paper registers or spreadsheets.

A salesman can access route-wise customers, review outlet and transaction information, view the digital product catalogue, create new customer or outlet records with a map location, record daily activities and send completed field data to the SalesOn web platform. Specialist SalesOn modules add detailed order booking, GPS tracking, payment collection, attendance and target workflows.
