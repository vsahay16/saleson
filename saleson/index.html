What is SalesOn?

SalesOn is sales and distribution software for MSMEs, distributors, manufacturers and field sales teams. It connects mobile field work with office records across orders, stock, billing and collections.

Can SalesOn work with our existing Tally setup?

SalesOn supports Tally integration. Discuss your version, records and required direction of transfer in a demo. The Tally integration page explains the supported workflows.

Does the mobile app work offline?

No. SalesOn requires an internet connection and does not support offline use. The Mobile App page has download links and device compatibility guidance.

Do we need to move every workflow at once?

Use the demo to choose the workflows your business needs and plan the account setup. Product data, opening information, access and training requirements determine the rollout.

Can a manufacturer use SalesOn?

Yes. Manufacturers can explore production and BOM workflows alongside sales and distribution. Production and van sales are Enterprise workflows; check plan inclusions before subscribing.

Where can an existing customer get help?

Use your assigned login to access SalesOn. For account help, contact support@saleson.co.in or +91 8092856577 during working hours. Do not share your password with support.
