The Problem

Why Outstanding Keeps Growing
Despite Constant Follow-Up

Without invoice-wise tracking, distributors chase totals instead of the right invoices. The team needs the invoice details and latest receipt history before following up.

📊
No invoice-level visibility

Party balance shows a lump sum. You can't tell which invoice is 45 days old and which is 90+ overdue — follow-up becomes guesswork.

💳
Credit limits ignored at order time

Salesman books an order. Accounts discovers three days later the party was already over limit. Outstanding compounds.

🔗
Every receipt entered twice

Agent records in a register. Accounts re-enters separately. Mismatches surface at month-end.

📋 Feature Module 1

Invoice-wise Outstanding —
Know Every Rupee Owed

See outstanding by invoice — not just party total. Every rupee is aged into 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, and 90+ day buckets so your team chases the right accounts first.

  • Invoice-level aging: 0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ day buckets per party. Prioritise the oldest, largest first.
  • Partial payment tracking: Outstanding reduces on each payment. Remaining invoices stay open until fully settled.
  • Agent assignment: Assign outstanding to field agents by area or route — clear ownership per collection.
  • Party account view: Full history per customer — invoices, payments, receipts — in one screen.
