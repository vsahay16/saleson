When to shortlist Delta Sales App or SalesOn

Delta’s official workflow includes mobile attendance, orders, expenses and collections, with web-based manager review. Its website also lists distributor management, van sales and retailer ordering. [1] [2]

Shortlist SalesOn when published INR pricing, ₹0 setup and a two-user entry point matter alongside order-to-office operations. Keep Delta in the evaluation when its rep, retailer and manager workflows match your needs. Do not assume either system wins every field-sales task.

Delta Sales App comparison questions

Is Delta only a tracking app?

No. Its official pages describe orders, collections, expenses, distributor operations and retailer workflows. Compare the implementation and included scope rather than assuming these functions are absent.

Can SalesOn work without internet on rural routes?

No. SalesOn requires an internet connection. If offline work is essential, treat that as a selection requirement and test the other provider’s exact offline scope.
