Bring the same buying brief to each provider

  • List users by role and ask which accounts are billable.
  • Separate required operations, CRM processes, AI features and offline work.
  • Ask for the edition, setup, integrations, support scope and billing term in writing.
  • Compare the amount due now, a 12-month cost and any minimum contract commitment separately.

Free CRM plans can suit contact and deal tracking; check whether the required distribution workflow is included. SalesOn offers operational workflows with minimum 2 users, rather than claiming to replace every CRM or advanced AI platform.
