How to write a polite payment reminder message

A good payment reminder is professional, clear and easy to act on. Follow these principles:

  • ✓ Address the customer by name — makes it personal, not generic
  • ✓ Mention the specific invoice number and amount — no ambiguity
  • ✓ Include your payment details — UPI, bank or payment link
  • ✓ Keep first reminders polite and short — escalate gradually
  • ✓ Always ask for a confirmed payment date if not paying immediately
  • ✓ End with a thank-you or appreciation line for good relationship
  • ✓ For long overdue, be firm but never rude or threatening
