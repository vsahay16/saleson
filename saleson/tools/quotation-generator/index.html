How to use this Quotation Generator

Create a professional business quotation in under 5 minutes — no account or software needed.

Step 1 — Fill Your Business Details

Enter your company name, address, GSTIN and contact details. Upload your logo for a professional branded look.

Step 2 — Add Customer Details

Enter the customer's name, company, address and GSTIN. The quotation preview updates live as you type.

Step 3 — Add Items and Pricing

Add each product or service with quantity, rate, discount and GST percentage. Tax amounts calculate automatically.

Step 4 — Set Validity and Terms

Set the validity period (7, 15, 30 or 45 days), payment terms, advance percentage and add scope of work notes.

Step 5 — Download or Share

Click Download PDF, print as A4, or share via WhatsApp. All formatting is handled — you get a clean professional document.

Step 6 — Convert to Invoice

Once the customer approves, click Convert to Invoice and open the GST Invoice Generator — your quotation data is pre-filled.

What should a professional quotation include?

A complete quotation helps customers make faster purchase decisions and reduces back-and-forth.

Company Details

Your business name, address, GSTIN, phone and email. A logo adds professional credibility.

Customer Details

Customer name, address and GSTIN. Necessary for GST compliance and record keeping.

Quotation Number & Date

A unique reference number and issue date for tracking and follow-up.

Validity Date

Until when the prices are locked. Creates urgency and protects you from price changes.

Itemised Pricing with GST

Each item with rate, quantity, discount, GST percentage, taxable value and total.

Payment Terms

Advance percentage, credit period, payment method. Prevents payment disputes.

Quotation vs Estimate vs Proforma Invoice vs GST Invoice

Document Purpose Legal Status
QuotationFormal price offer for products/servicesNon-binding offer
EstimateApproximate cost indicationInformal, approximate
Proforma InvoicePre-invoice to confirm order detailsNot a GST document
GST InvoiceOfficial tax document after supplyLegal GST document
