How to use this GST Calculator

Step 1 — Select Mode

Choose Add GST to calculate total from base price, Remove GST to extract tax from inclusive price, or Invoice Total for multi-item bills.

Step 2 — Enter Amount

Enter the base amount. Use the quick rate buttons to set GST rate (5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) or select from the dropdown.

Step 3 — Choose GST Type

Select Intra-State for same-state supply (CGST + SGST split) or Inter-State for IGST.

Step 4 — Add Optional Fields

Add quantity, discount or shipping charges if needed. Toggle round-off as required.

Step 5 — See Result

Results show instantly — taxable value, CGST, SGST, IGST, grand total and amount in words.

Step 6 — Copy or Use in Invoice

Copy the calculation or click Use in GST Invoice to carry these values into the SalesOn GST Invoice Generator.

How to add GST to an amount

To find the final price after adding GST to a base amount:

Formula — Add GST

GST Amount = Base Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100
Grand Total = Base Amount + GST Amount

Example: ₹10,000 × 18% = ₹1,800 GST → Total = ₹11,800

How to remove GST from a GST-inclusive price

To find the taxable value when you only have the inclusive price (Reverse GST):

Formula — Remove GST (Reverse Calculation)

Taxable Value = Inclusive Price × 100 ÷ (100 + GST Rate)
GST Amount = Inclusive Price − Taxable Value

Example: ₹11,800 inclusive at 18% → Taxable = ₹10,000 → GST = ₹1,800

CGST, SGST & IGST — what is the difference?

Tax Full Form When Applied Rate
CGSTCentral GSTIntra-state supplyHalf of GST rate
SGSTState GSTIntra-state supplyHalf of GST rate
IGSTIntegrated GSTInter-state supplyFull GST rate

Example at 18% GST: Intra-state → CGST 9% + SGST 9%. Inter-state → IGST 18%.
