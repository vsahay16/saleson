Test these tasks during a demo

  • Build an order with your products, units, prices and scheme.
  • Correct a quantity and inspect the office handover.
  • Invoice the order, record a partial payment and check remaining dues.
  • If required, check godown stock and a Tally posting exception.
  • Test salesperson/manager access and request help with a failed task.
