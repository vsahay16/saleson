Everything About
E-Way Bills for GST

What is an E-Way Bill?

An e-way bill (Electronic Way Bill) is a document required under GST for movement of goods in India above a specified consignment value. It must be generated on the e-way bill portal before goods leave the origin location.

When is it Required?

An e-way bill is generally required when the consignment value exceeds ₹50,000. This applies to all movement of taxable goods — whether for sale, stock transfer, job work, van loading, purchase return or delivery before invoice.

Documents Needed for E-Way Bill

To generate an e-way bill you need: supplier and recipient GSTIN, document number (invoice / challan), HSN code, consignment value, vehicle number, transporter ID, and from/to locations.

Delivery Challan vs Invoice vs E-Way Bill

A delivery challan is the dispatch document for goods movement without a sale (van loading, job work, stock transfer). A GST invoice records the sale. An e-way bill is the transit permit required for both when value exceeds ₹50,000.

E-Way Bill for Stock Transfer & Van Loading

Stock transfer and van loading require a delivery challan as the base document. If the consignment value exceeds ₹50,000, an e-way bill is also required — even though no sale has occurred. Some states have different rules.

E-Way Bill for Job Work

When goods are sent to a job worker for processing, an e-way bill is typically required if the value exceeds ₹50,000. Special exemptions apply for small job work under certain conditions — verify with your accountant.
