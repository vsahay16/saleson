Quick Answers

Tally Integration — Plainly Explained

Connect field sales and office accounting. Use the walkthrough to review how your customer masters, items and sales transactions should move between SalesOn and Tally.

What is Tally integration software?

Software that connects a distributor's field app to Tally Prime or ERP 9 via Tally's native XML/HTTP API. When a rep confirms an invoice in the field, the voucher posts automatically — correct ledger, GST rate, and HSN included. No imports, no WhatsApp, no re-entry.

What syncs between SalesOn and Tally?

Field → Tally: Sales vouchers, payment receipts, new customer ledgers.
Tally → Field: Item masters, prices, HSN codes, outstanding balances.
All sync is event-based and real-time — not batch, not scheduled.

Does SalesOn replace Tally?

No. Your accountant keeps working in Tally exactly as before. SalesOn only appends new field transactions via the configured Tally integration — no existing vouchers, ledgers, or configurations are touched.
